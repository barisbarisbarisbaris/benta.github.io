Debt Quest - Hellavision Television Network Presents

October 18 - 19, 2024

DEBT QUEST is an animated action-adventure RPG created by Lije Morgan. It is a hybrid Story / Open Call episode of Hellavision Television, featuring contributions from various animators. The plot follows a party embarking on a journey to distant worlds, slaying enemies and paying off their debts along the way.

An in-person screening was held in Istanbul at BENTA on October 18th and 19th, alongside screenings in Atlanta, New York City, Los Angeles, London, Toronto, Minneapolis and Chicago.
